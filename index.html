PUBLIC PROFILE · RORY :P · SHE/HER · MODEL“to break barriers, to reach for the stars 🐰”

My name is Aurora, and I’m a model, creative, and visual storyteller based in Russia. For me, modeling has never been only about standing in front of a camera. It is a way to explore identity, fashion, emotion, and the different sides of my personality.

HOW IT STARTED

I discovered my interest in modeling at a young age and started experimenting with it when I was around 15. My journey began quite unexpectedly: my first professional photoshoot happened after I was approached by a photographer on the street. That experience made me realize that I genuinely enjoyed being in front of the camera, and it encouraged me to take modeling more seriously. After that, I began working on TFP projects, building my portfolio and learning how to create images that actually feel like me.

THE WHOLE IMAGE

Since then, I’ve become increasingly involved not only as a model, but also as a creative part of the process. In many of my projects, I choose the location, develop the concept, create my own styling, and do my own makeup and hair. I’m especially passionate about fashion because I see clothing and beauty as a form of self-expression rather than simply something aesthetic. I also edit and retouch many of my own photographs, which allows me to stay involved from the first idea all the way to the final image.

PERSONAL STYLE

One of the biggest parts of my personal style is gyaru. It is the aesthetic I feel most connected to and the style I naturally wear in my everyday life. Because of that, some of my favorite shoots are the ones where I can bring this part of myself into the concept. At the same time, I don’t want to limit myself to one aesthetic. I enjoy experimenting with editorial fashion, portraits, street photography, darker concepts, feminine styling, unusual locations, and completely different visual moods.

BEYOND MODELING

I’m naturally very curious and tend to become deeply involved in anything that interests me. Outside of modeling, I’m passionate about astrophysics and technology, and I have been interested in programming since I was young. I also enjoy drawing, dancing, sports, fashion, photography, and other creative projects. I like learning new things and constantly looking for ways to develop myself, whether that means improving my English, learning something technical, experimenting with a new visual style, or simply trying something I have never done before.

COLLABORATION

I think this curiosity is also a big part of how I approach modeling. I’m not interested in simply reproducing the same image over and over again. I want to understand the concept, contribute ideas, experiment, and see how far a creative direction can be taken. I especially enjoy working with people who have their own vision and are open to collaboration.

A LIVING ARCHIVE

My portfolio is therefore not just a collection of photographs. It is a record of how I have grown creatively and personally. Every shoot represents a different period, idea, or side of me — from my very first professional experience, to my early studio work, to self-organized gyaru shoots and collaborations with photographers and other creatives.

WHAT COMES NEXT

I’m still at the beginning of my professional modeling journey, and I see that as something exciting rather than limiting. There is still so much I want to learn, experience, and create. I want to continue developing my skills, work with new photographers and creative teams, explore different areas of fashion and editorial photography, and eventually take part in larger productions.

Most importantly, I want my work to feel authentic. I want the people looking through my portfolio to see not only a model, but a person with her own ideas, interests, personality, and perspective.

This is me — still learning, still experimenting, and constantly creating.
